WHO WE ARE

Leadership Starts With Us.

The HPS Student Lighthouse Team is a student leadership initiative focused on responsibility, teamwork, service and positive change.

We believe leadership is not just about having a title. It is about taking responsibility, listening to others, helping our community and setting a positive example through our actions. Together, we aim to make our school a place where every student has the opportunity to grow and lead.

◎

Our Mission

Encourage students to take initiative, serve others and become responsible leaders.

✦

Our Vision

Build a school community where every voice matters and positive change begins with us.

♡

Our Values

Honesty, respect, responsibility, teamwork and continuous improvement.

The 7 Habits of Effective Leadership

Small daily choices help us become better learners, stronger teammates and more effective leaders.

01

Be Proactive

Take responsibility for your choices.

02

Begin With the End in Mind

Know your goals before you begin.

03

Put First Things First

Focus on what matters most.

04

Think Win-Win

Look for solutions that benefit everyone.

05

Seek First to Understand

Listen before you respond.

06

Synergize

Work together and value differences.

07

Sharpen the Saw

Keep learning and improving yourself.
